2. Purpose of the Site

HIBCW is a non-emergency community crime-awareness tool that helps Hawaii Island businesses and residents share information about local incidents. It is not an emergency service and is not monitored around the clock.

⚠ FOR EMERGENCIES, CALL 911 IMMEDIATELY. Do not use this site to report crimes in progress or situations involving an immediate threat to life or safety.

A report on HIBCW is not an official police report and does not notify the Hawaiʻi Police Department (HPD). To file a police report, call HPD’s non-emergency line at (808) 935-3311 or visit hawaiipolice.gov. For anonymous tips, call Crime Stoppers at (808) 961-8300.

3. Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years old to submit an incident report, apply for membership, or register a business. Anyone may view the public map and incident list.

4. Accuracy of Reports

By submitting a report, you confirm that:

  • The information is true and accurate to the best of your knowledge, and based on your own observation or direct knowledge.
  • You are not submitting a false, misleading, fabricated, or malicious report.
  • Any suspect description is based on what you personally saw, not on assumptions, stereotypes, or secondhand information.
  • You are not targeting anyone out of malice, a personal dispute, or discriminatory intent.

KIAA may edit, remove, or archive any report it decides, in its sole discretion, is false, misleading, defamatory, harassing, off-topic, or submitted in bad faith.

5. Protecting People’s Privacy in Reports

Reports are public. Describe what happened, not who you think someone is.

  • Do not name or accuse a private individual unless they have been publicly charged, or the information comes from an official police source.
  • Do not post anyone’s home address, phone number, or other personal details.
  • Avoid photos or videos that clearly show bystanders, children, or the inside of private homes when they are not needed to describe the incident.
  • Do not include Social Security numbers, financial account details, medical information, or similar sensitive data.

6. Membership and the Verified Member Badge

KIAA members can apply to join HIBCW. KIAA reviews every application and may approve or decline it at its discretion. Approved members may receive district alert emails, and reports submitted with an email address on the KIAA member list show a “Verified KIAA Member” badge.

The badge means only that the email address matches a KIAA member. It does not mean KIAA or law enforcement has checked the report. Do not use another person’s email address to obtain the badge.

7. Business Registration

Businesses can register with HIBCW so KIAA and District Captains know who to contact in each district. You must be authorized to register the business, and the information you give must be accurate. Registration details are not shown on the public map.

8. District Captains and District Alerts

District Captains are volunteers approved by KIAA. A captain’s name, district, and (if provided) email and photo are shown publicly so members know who to contact.

KIAA staff and District Captains may send alert emails about safety issues to approved members in a district. Captains may only send alerts to their own district. Alerts must be about community safety and must follow the No Advertising Policy below. Members may ask to stop receiving alerts at any time by emailing hibcw@kiaahilo.org.
