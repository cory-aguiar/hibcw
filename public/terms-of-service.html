3. HIPAA and protected health information — important notice

⚠ This platform is NOT a HIPAA-covered platform and does not process PHI.

KIAA Connect is a benefits administration tool, not a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse. The Platform is not designed, configured, or intended to receive, store, transmit, or process Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined under the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (HIPAA) and its implementing regulations (45 CFR Parts 160 and 164).

3.1 What constitutes PHI

PHI includes any individually identifiable health information, including but not limited to:

  • Medical diagnoses, conditions, or treatment records
  • Prescription drug information tied to an individual
  • Health insurance claim information linked to an identifiable individual
  • Any information that could identify a specific individual and relates to their physical or mental health, healthcare services received, or payment for healthcare

3.2 Prohibited uploads and submissions

You must not upload, submit, enter, or transmit PHI through KIAA Connect under any circumstances. This prohibition applies to all areas of the Platform, including but not limited to:

  • Document uploads (company documents, carrier documents, plan documents)
  • Open enrollment forms and elections
  • Employee headcount and FTE worksheets — enter aggregate numbers only, not individual employee health data
  • Notes, tasks, or any free-text fields
  • Company profile fields

3.3 Permitted information

The Platform is designed to handle only the following categories of information, none of which constitute PHI:

  • Employer/group-level enrollment elections (plan selections, contribution amounts)
  • Aggregate employee headcounts and FTE figures
  • Employer contact information (HR contacts, company address)
  • Plan documents, SBCs, and compliance notices that apply to all employees uniformly
  • Group-level compliance status (COBRA applicability, FMLA applicability, ERISA filing status)

3.4 Individual employee enrollment

Individual employee enrollment and member-level data — including individual subscribers, dependents, dates of birth, and plan elections at the individual level — are handled directly through HMSA and Kaiser Permanente enrollment systems, not through KIAA Connect. Do not enter individual employee health plan enrollment data into this Platform.
