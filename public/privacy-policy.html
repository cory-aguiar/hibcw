Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 26, 2026  ·  Last updated: May 26, 2026
Plain language summary: KIAA Connect collects only the minimum information needed to administer group health benefits for KIAA member employers. We do not collect, store, or process Protected Health Information (PHI). We do not sell your data. We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to operate the platform.

1. Introduction

The Kanoelehua Industrial Area Association ("KIAA," "we," "us," or "our") operates KIAA Connect, a benefits administration platform located at connect.kiaahilo.org ("the Platform"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the Platform.

By using the Platform, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, please do not use the Platform.

2. HIPAA and protected health information

⚠ KIAA Connect does not collect, store, or process Protected Health Information (PHI).

This Platform is a benefits administration tool that handles group-level enrollment elections and compliance documents. It is not a healthcare provider, health plan, or healthcare clearinghouse, and is not subject to HIPAA as a covered entity or business associate with respect to the data it collects through this Platform.

Users must not upload PHI to this Platform. Individual employee health information, medical records, diagnoses, claim data, or any other individually identifiable health information must never be entered into or uploaded to KIAA Connect. See our Terms of Service for the full prohibition and consequences of violations.

Individual employee enrollment and member-level data are handled directly through HMSA and Kaiser Permanente secure enrollment portals, which maintain their own HIPAA-compliant privacy and security programs.

3. Information we collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide benefits administration services to KIAA member employers. We do not collect individual employee health data.

3.1 Information you provide

CategoryWhat we collectPurpose
Account information Name, work email address, phone number, job title, password (hashed) Account creation and authentication
Company information Company name, business address, HR contact details, benefits contact details, HMSA group number, company code Benefits administration and communication
Enrollment elections Group-level plan selections, employer contribution amounts, plan year elections Facilitating open enrollment with HMSA/Kaiser
Workforce data Aggregate employee headcounts (full-time, part-time, seasonal), FTE calculations, enrolled employee counts PHCA, COBRA, FMLA, and ERISA compliance thresholds — aggregate only, no individual employee data

Company logo Logo image file uploaded by the company Display on employee-facing plan portal

3.2 Information collected automatically

When you use the Platform, we and our service providers may automatically collect:

  • Log data (IP address, browser type, pages visited, time and date of access)
  • Authentication events (login timestamps, session information)

This information is used for security monitoring, troubleshooting, and improving the Platform. It is not used for advertising purposes.

4. How we use your information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain KIAA Connect
  • Facilitate open enrollment and plan elections between member employers and HMSA/Kaiser
  • Generate required compliance documents (SPDs, COBRA notices, FMLA notices)
  • Communicate with HR contacts regarding plan administration
  • Maintain records required by ERISA, PHCA, and other applicable laws
  • Secure the Platform and investigate unauthorized access or misuse
  • Improve the Platform's features and functionality

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to any third party for commercial purposes.

5. Information sharing

We share information only in the following limited circumstances:

5.1 Carriers and administrators

Group enrollment elections and related plan administration data are shared with Hawaii Medical Service Association (HMSA) and Kaiser Permanente as necessary to process enrollment and administer group health plans. This sharing is necessary to provide the services you have requested and is governed by agreements between KIAA and the respective carriers.

5.2 Service providers

We use the following service providers to operate the Platform:

  • Supabase — database hosting and authentication (data stored in U.S.-based infrastructure)
  • Vercel — application hosting and deployment
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (account registration, password reset)

Each service provider is contractually obligated to protect your information and use it only for the purpose of providing services to KIAA.

5.3 Legal requirements

We may disclose information if required to do so by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of KIAA, our users, or the public.

5.4 Business transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of KIAA's operations, information held by the Platform may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify affected users of any such transfer.

6. Data retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the Platform's services and to comply with applicable legal obligations, including ERISA record-keeping requirements (generally six years for plan-related records). When information is no longer needed, we delete or anonymize it.

When you or your company terminates use of the Platform, we will retain records for the minimum period required by law and then delete them upon request.

7. Data security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect information stored on and transmitted through the Platform, including:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage of passwords (bcrypt hashing via Supabase Auth)
  • Row-level security policies restricting database access to authorized users only
  • Role-based access controls (KIAA staff vs. HR client vs. employee-facing portal)
  • Supabase infrastructure with SOC 2 Type II compliance

While we take security seriously, no system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your account credentials.

8. Your rights

As a user of the Platform, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correction — update or correct inaccurate information through your profile settings or by contacting us
  • Deletion — request deletion of your account and associated personal information, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Portability — request an export of your company's data in a standard format

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at admin@kiaahilo.org or (808) 961-5422.

9. Cookies and tracking

The Platform uses only essential cookies required for authentication and session management. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics services. No cross-site tracking occurs.

10. Children's privacy

The Platform is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If you believe a minor has provided personal information through the Platform, please contact us immediately.

11. Hawaii privacy law

KIAA operates under the laws of the State of Hawaii. We comply with applicable Hawaii privacy laws, including HRS Chapter 487N (Security Breach of Personal Information) and other applicable state privacy requirements. In the event of a security breach involving your personal information, we will notify affected individuals as required by Hawaii law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify registered users of material changes via email or through the Platform. The date of the most recent update is shown at the top of this page. Your continued use of the Platform after any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy.
